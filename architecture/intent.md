# Intent

## One sentence

Help a lawyer move from matter context to source-grounded research, document review and a reviewable draft faster — without handing legal authority, confidentiality decisions or consequential actions to the model.

## Primary user

**Bao — real GitLaw Pro pilot lawyer.**

The same product contract should generalise to other law-firm pilot users without pretending every practice area has identical workflows.

## Problem

Legal knowledge work is not just "answer a legal question". A lawyer repeatedly has to:

- reconstruct the matter and missing facts;
- inspect client or case documents;
- find the relevant legal sources;
- distinguish evidence from generated interpretation;
- prepare drafts;
- review the result before anything consequential leaves the firm.

Generic AI can make this faster while simultaneously creating unacceptable failure modes: unsupported claims, broken citations, hidden uncertainty, leaked confidential information, or the appearance that an assistant has legal authority it does not possess.

## Desired outcome

GitLaw Pro becomes a trustworthy legal workbench in which Bao can complete three repeatable jobs:

1. understand a matter and research the relevant law;
2. review a case document and inspect the supporting sources;
3. turn grounded research into a draft that remains blocked until a lawyer explicitly reviews and releases it.

Corrections from real use should become product changes, regressions and better golden cases rather than disappearing into chat history.

## Non-goals

- Replacing Bao's legal judgement.
- Autonomous consequential legal decisions or external legal actions.
- Treating a synthetic demo as proof of real law-firm production reliability.
- Publishing confidential pilot matters or raw pilot feedback as portfolio evidence.
- Claiming complete legal-answer accuracy across German law.

## Success looks like

Bao can use the product on realistic work without having to trust an opaque answer. He can always identify the sources, missing facts, uncertainty and review state; consequential output cannot pass the lawyer-authority boundary silently.

For public proof, a reviewer should be able to understand those boundaries and exercise equivalent synthetic golden cases without access to Bao's private matters.
