## 1. Erste Verordnung zur Änderung der Vorsorgeregister-Verordnung — GEÄNDERT

- **BGBl.-Fundstelle:** BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 238 vom 25.08.2026
- **Ausfertigungsdatum:** 17.08.2026
- **FNA:** 303-1-1 · Federführung: Bundesministerium der Justiz und für Verbraucherschutz
- **Sachgebiet:** Notare, Rechtsanwälte, Rechtsberater; Beurkundung
- **Inkrafttreten:** Verkündet am 25.08.2026; das genaue Inkrafttretensdatum steht in der Schlussvorschrift des Regelungstext-PDF (nicht maschinell extrahiert — bitte beim Regenerieren des Explainers prüfen)
- **Bürger-Impact:** Betrifft die Registrierung von Vorsorgevollmachten und Betreuungsverfügungen im Zentralen Vorsorgeregister der Bundesnotarkammer — geänderte Melde-/Eintragungsmodalitäten für alle, die eine Vorsorgevollmacht hinterlegt haben oder hinterlegen wollen.
- **Korpus-Abgleich:** `laws/vregv.md` vorhanden → Datei ist jetzt VERALTET, muss neu geparst werden.
- **Musterbrief-Relevanz:** Vorsorgevollmacht-/Betreuungs-Musterbriefe prüfen.

- **Ausfertigungsdatum:** 14.08.2026

- **Gegenstand:** Festlegung von Flugverfahren für An- und Abflüge nach Instrumentenflugregeln zum und vom Verkehrslandeplatz Allendorf/Eder

### EN
- **Headline:** Law-Watch 30 Aug 2026 — 2 new, 2 amended federal regulations (BGBl. I No. 238–241)
- **What changed:** Four Federal Law Gazette Part I publications appeared in the window 24–30 Aug 2026. Amended: (1) First Regulation amending the Advance-Directive Register Regulation, BGBl. 2026 I No. 238 of 25.08.2026, signed 17.08.2026, FNA 303-1-1, lead ministry Federal Ministry of Justice and Consumer Protection, subject area notaries/lawyers/legal advisers and notarisation; (2) Regulation amending the Regulation on the Register of Company Base Data, BGBl. 2026 I No. 240 of 27.08.2026, signed 17.08.2026, subject area statistics. New: (3) BAS Final Examination Regulation (BASAPrV) — Regulation on conducting the final examination in the state-recognised public-service training occupations within the remit of the Federal Office for Social Security, BGBl. 2026 I No. 239 of 26.08.2026, signed 21.08.2026, subject area vocational training; (4) Regulation replacing the 226th Implementing Regulation to the Air Traffic Order (setting instrument-flight-rules arrival and departure procedures to and from Allendorf/Eder airfield), BGBl. 2026 I No. 241 of 28.08.2026, signed 14.08.2026, subject area air traffic.
- **Why:** GitLaw's corpus of 5,984 laws in `laws/` must never serve stale law to citizens. `laws/vregv.md` and `laws/ubregv.md` (UBRegV, signed 01.07.2024) already exist and are now outdated; items 3 and 4 have no match in the corpus and must be added.
- **Effective:** No. 238 promulgated 25.08.2026; No. 239 promulgated 26.08.2026; No. 240 promulgated 27.08.2026; No. 241 promulgated 28.08.2026. The exact entry-into-force dates are in the final provisions of each regulation's PDF and were not machine-extracted — please verify when regenerating the explainers.
- **Deadline:** No citizen filing deadline is triggered by these four publications. Maintainer action deadline: re-parse before the next explainer regeneration run.
- **Who's affected:** No. 238 — everyone who has lodged or wants to lodge an advance directive or care directive in the Central Advance-Directive Register of the Federal Chamber of Notaries. No. 240 — self-employed people, freelancers and companies whose base data are stored in the company base data register and shared between authorities (once-only principle). No. 239 — trainees in public-service occupations at the Federal Office for Social Security and their training bodies. No. 241 — residents around Allendorf/Eder airfield (aircraft noise), locally relevant only.
- **How:** Re-parse `laws/vregv.md` and `laws/ubregv.md` from the official texts, add BASAPrV and the new Air Traffic Order implementing regulation as new corpus entries, and review the advance-directive/guardianship Musterbriefe among the 31 templates.
- **Citizen tip:** If you hold an advance directive (Vorsorgevollmacht) registered with the Central Advance-Directive Register, read BGBl. 2026 I No. 238 before your next update — registration and notification formalities have changed. Item 4 is a low-priority regional regulation.
