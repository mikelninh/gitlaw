- **Inkrafttreten:** 01.01.2027
- **Bürgerwirkung (1 Zeile):** Landwirtschaftliche Betriebe mit Agrarstatistik-Meldungen erhalten angepasste Erhebungspflichten (EU-Rechtsangleichung) — wirksam ab dem Referenzjahr 2027.

### EN
- **Headline:** Second Ordinance Amending the Agricultural Statistics Ordinance
- **What changed:** Updates the Agricultural Statistics Ordinance (AgrStatV) to transpose amended EU agricultural‑statistics law; adjusts which holdings must report and what data is collected.
- **Why:** Implementation of Union law (EU agricultural‑statistics reform) plus administrative simplification.
- **Effective:** 1 January 2027
- **Deadline:** First affected reporting for the 2027 reference year.
- **Who's affected:** Agricultural holdings and farms subject to agri‑statistics reporting.
- **How:** Via the amended Agricultural Statistics Ordinance; reporting obligations adjusted from 2027.
- **Citizen tip:** If you run a farm subject to statistical reporting, check the 2027 reporting requirements early — thresholds and items change.

### EN
- **Headline:** Ordinance Amending the Minimum Tax Reporting Ordinance
- **What changed:** Amends the Minimum Tax Reporting Ordinance (MinStBV) implementing the EU Minimum Tax Directive (Pillar Two / global minimum tax); expands country‑by‑country and minimum‑tax reporting.
- **Why:** Transposition of the EU Directive on minimum taxation of large groups (Pillar Two).
- **Effective:** 18 August 2026 (day after promulgation)
- **Deadline:** Affected groups must file minimum‑tax reports for fiscal years beginning in or after 2026.
- **Who's affected:** Large multinational / concerned groups subject to the Minimum Tax Act (MinStG).
- **How:** Via the amended Minimum Tax Reporting Ordinance; reporting obligations expanded.
- **Citizen tip:** If you advise or run a large group, align your 2026 minimum‑tax reporting with the new rules now.

## 4. Verordnung zur Anpassung der GAP-Direktzahlungen-Verordnung, der GAPInVeKoS-Verordnung und der GAP-Konditionalitäten-Verordnung (BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 237)

  - https://www.buzer.de/gesetz/17645/index.htm

- **What changed:** Adapts the CAP Direct Payments Ordinance (GAPDZV), the CAP InVeKoS Ordinance and the CAP Conditionality Ordinance to Regulation (EU) 2025/2649 and provides relief for agricultural holdings (e.g. simplified checks).
- **Why:** Transposition of the EU CAP reform regulation and reduction of administrative burden for farmers.
- **Effective:** 19 August 2026
- **Deadline:** Apply updated rules for the relevant payment year.
- **Who's affected:** Farmers and agricultural holdings receiving EU/CAP direct payments and eco‑schemes.
- **How:** Via the amended CAP ordinances; relief and adjusted obligations from 19.08.2026.
- **Citizen tip:** If you receive direct payments, review the 2026 relief measures (e.g. simplified on‑site checks) with your paying agency.
