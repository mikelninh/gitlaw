# GitLaw Wiki

Welcome to the GitLaw project wiki. This is the technical companion to [README.md](https://github.com/mikelninh/gitlaw/blob/main/README.md).

## What is GitLaw

A two-tier German legal-tech platform built by [Mikel Ninh](https://github.com/mikelninh) in Berlin:

- **GitLaw** (free, open-source for citizens): semantic search over 5,936 German federal laws with AI-explained paragraphs
- **GitLaw Pro** (closed beta for lawyers): mandate workflow, RAG-grounded research with citation verification, branded letter templates, status workflow, multilingual client communication

Live: [gitlaw-xi.vercel.app](https://gitlaw-xi.vercel.app/)
