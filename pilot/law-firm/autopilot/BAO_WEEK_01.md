# Bao Week 01 — measured workload experiment

## Goal

Identify and remove the largest repeatable sources of lawyer/team time in the real Kanzlei workflow without increasing unsafe autonomy.

## Success condition

Week 01 is successful only when:

```text
confirmed lawyer minutes saved > 0
confirmed staff minutes saved  > 0
useful prepared outputs         > 0
unauthorized external actions   = 0
silent deadline confirmations   = 0
cross-matter access             = 0
```

## Sample

Start with 10–20 ordinary matters that represent normal workload. Prefer synthetic/redacted replay first; real mandate data only after the Kanzlei's production data/confidentiality gates are explicitly complete.

## Measure these workflows

For each sampled matter record the **actual active-work minutes** before Autopilot and after Autopilot:

1. first intake reconstruction;
2. document classification/review preparation;
3. missing-document follow-up;
4. case/timeline reconstruction;
5. legal research;
6. first draft;
7. routine client status communication;
8. deadline review;
9. billing preparation.

Do not replace measurement with estimates after the experiment starts.

## Daily Bao interview — maximum 5 minutes

Only ask:

1. What did GitLaw save you from doing today?
2. What did you still have to redo?
3. What did it surface too late?
4. What should it have handled without you?
5. Was anything unsafe or annoying?

Turn each answer into either a workflow change, regression test, or explicit non-goal.

## Priority rule

Build the next automation by:

`weekly minutes wasted × repeatability × safe-automation potential`

Do not prioritize based on demo attractiveness.

## Initial hypothesis order

1. missing-document chasing;
2. document intake / OCR / classification;
3. intake reconstruction;
4. what-changed / timeline preparation;
5. routine status updates;
6. research packets;
7. first drafts;
8. deadline evidence/review;
9. billing preparation.

This order is a hypothesis until Bao's measured week replaces it.

## End-of-week report

Publish privately first:

- sampled matters;
- confirmed baseline minutes;
- confirmed after minutes;
- lawyer hours returned;
- staff hours returned;
- correction rate;
- review/approval count;
- blocked unsafe actions;
- failures/reconciliation;
- top remaining bottleneck;
- next autonomy change, if any.

Do not publish client identities or matter details.
